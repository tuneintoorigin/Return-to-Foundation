Return to Foundation

Your foundation is who you already are underneath the fear, the striving, and the old wounds — the truth God placed in you before anything ever tried to convince you otherwise. Every emotion that moves through you is information, not identity. It's never meant to be believed as fact. It's meant to be brought into the light and exchanged for truth.

When an emotion starts running your life — when you're spiraling, can't let it go, can't move forward — the goal isn't to suppress it or become it. It's to notice the lie it's telling you in that moment, and exchange that lie for what God actually says. This is healing through Christ consciousness: grounded in the body, renewed in the mind.

"Be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind."

Romans 12:2
